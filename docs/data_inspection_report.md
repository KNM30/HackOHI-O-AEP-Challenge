## 7. Fields that may be shown to customers

Only fields classified **customer-safe** above reach the browser, through explicit response models (`backend/app/schemas.py`). In particular:

- Premises are shown by a salted-hash ID (`PRM-XXXXXXXX`), service type (Overhead / Underground), single vs multi-unit, and circuit label. Source premise numbers never leave the server.
- The map overview shows service transformers (equipment), not homes. The selected premise's own point is shown only for that premise.
- `critical_c` (life-support flag) is **never** used or exposed. Medical / accessibility needs come only from what the user declares in the app.
- Road names, city, street codes, house-number annotations, SCADA ids, global ids and raw location numbers are internal.
- Historical damage is labelled as historical, shown with date, asset and damage category, and its point is rounded to ~100 m for display.
- Hospital / fire / ambulance layers are public data but are not shown on the customer map; precise sensitive-facility locations are not exposed.
